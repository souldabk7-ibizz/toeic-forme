# Part 1 photographs

Drop a photo in here and name it after the item's `art` id in
`webapp/data/mock.js` — for example `office_typing.jpg` — then add
`"photo": "office_typing.jpg"` to that item. The app shows the photo and
ignores the drawing; items without a photo keep their drawing, so the two can
be mixed while a set is being filled in.

What makes a usable Part 1 photo:

- one clear subject doing one clear action, large in the frame
- a second object visible but idle — that is what the wrong options reach for
- no text or logos in shot, and nothing that identifies a real person
- landscape, around 800px wide is plenty; keep files under ~200KB
